# 14. Bulk drafts and the review queue

Status: built. How the local admin app (`06`) lets one person review many prepared replies quickly and safely: the agent prepares one draft per client with the reason for it (a phrase in a session, `bulk-drafts`), the person checks each one and sends it one at a time. Nothing here sends in bulk, and no route or status can send on its own.

## One queue
Drafts is the only place a draft is reviewed. A batch is a read-only view of it (`/drafts?batch=3`, in the View menu, group Batches); there is no separate bulk screen and nothing in the admin creates a batch: choosing the clients is the agent's (decision 0025). Since P85 a `bulk-drafts` run IS a batch: its first recorded draft makes the `batches` row (named by the owner's words, `intent custom`, `audience agent`, joined to the run by its `batchKey`) and every draft of the run carries its `batchId` (`drafts.record`, the only writer of `batches`). A batch draft is a Mail draft like any other: Checked reads its Mail text (no exemption), and in a batch every unacknowledged safety result blocks Checked. The list searches on the server (name, address, subject; the counts in the View menu never narrow by the search), shows "7 of 40 · 3 done, 1 sent, 14 to go", and below 640 px keeps one sticky line (View menu, search, Select). Clients the agent did not answer by email (a call to make, no reply needed, crossed off, unknown sender) are recorded with `kind` and an optional free-text `phone` (migration 0015) and listed read-only in the view "Not by email"; they never enter the reply queue, and nothing in that path sends. `hub dev` seed: `seed-cli --many 120` adds a long list for manual viewing.

## Per-client rule
Even in a batch every reply is individual. The owner's phrase gives a **brief**: the purpose and tone for the agent, never a body, and the hub renders no body from it. The `bulk-drafts` skill picks the clients from the project's Notion `clients` source as the phrase says, keeps only addresses `draft.recipients` admits, and reads each client's thread and profile first (`skills/triage-mail/references/per-client.md`). Every record of one run carries one `batchKey`, so the run is one session in Activity and `hub batch-check --batch <batchKey>` judges the set.

## Writing on the owner's initiative (P85)
A message to a client the owner decides to send ("напиши ему ещё раз", "напиши всем клиентам про …") is `bulk-drafts`, for one client or many, even when the client has not answered or our last mail was sent. Each draft is a new reply to the newest message of the client's latest thread, ours (in Sent) or theirs, so its `mailRef` is that message and it never shares one with the sent draft before it. `draft_write` takes `owner_request` (his words, verbatim) and `hub record-draft` takes `ownerRequest`: with them a message whose drafts are all finished gets a new draft, and the record is not a reply of the client (follow-ups stay as they are). A reply to our own message goes to that message's recipients (the writer sets them, as the door does). A client with no thread at all is not written to by the Hub (no new threads): the agent gives the text in chat. Every draft is then sent by its own click; there is no "send all" (decision 0011) and no cap beyond the existing guards (the run's 30, `already_sent_recently`, `draft.recipients`, Stop sending).

## The review card
- Up to three tones per draft (`drafts.tones`; short / standard / warm, keys 1 2 3); changing the tone clears Checked.
- Safety results are computed at read time from rules that are data (`SAFETY_KINDS` in `@hub/config`, for example `do_not_contact`, `cc_present`, `unknown_recipients`, `risky_word`, `wrong_thread`, `already_sent_recently`, and the evidence checks of what the draft was based on); defaults live in `@hub/config`, per-project overrides in `safety_rules`, and only acknowledgements are stored. A red result blocks Checked until acknowledged.
- Send is gated by "I read the checks"; the card, its states and keys are in `06`.

## Sent detection
A recorded draft (bulk or not, `hub record-draft`) that the person sends from Mail is found in the project's Sent mailbox (`mail.sent_mailbox`) through `osascript`, matched by the Message-ID of a Mail version the Hub recorded for it, by the thread it answers (In-Reply-To = its `mailRef`, sent after the draft was made, to the client), or by subject and recipient (never by its text, P65; never by its `mailRef` as its own id, and never by a Sent message already attributed to another draft, P85); the server reads only while drafts wait, only since the oldest waiting one, and never launches Mail: when Mail is not running the loop is off and says so (decision 0026). `drafts.markSent` is the only writer of `sent`. Not built: a per-batch sent log screen (Activity's Sends view shows the send history).

## Data (SQLite, `packages/db`)
`batches` (audience, intent, brief, the picked and the excluded clients; read by the batch view), `drafts` (status `ready hold done sent skipped`, the `checked` flag, `tones`, `reason`, `safety` and `acked`, `mail_ref`, `batch_id`; one open draft per client, a partial unique index), `safety_rules`, `send_log`. Marks are the client marks of `06`.
