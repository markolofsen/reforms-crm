# Sequence drafts (the due follow-up steps, draft-only, one project)

> Layer: **core** (universal). Names in this file are examples; one owner's facts live in `projects/<slug>/` (custom). Rule: `@docs/LAYERS.md`. The engine: `@docs/core/23-sequences.md`.

Rules: `skills/README.md` ("Rules"). Talk to the owner in Russian, briefly. Manual-first: the owner started this session and said what he wants. **You enrol only on his words in this session** (step 2): never on your own initiative, never from a candidate list alone, never because a client looks quiet. **You never run `hub sequences pause` or `resume`** (they are the owner's: denied, and the hub refuses them in a session). Besides enrolling on his words you may only reduce mail: `stop`, `delay`, `judge`. The result shows in the admin by itself: the step drafts in Drafts (View "Follow-ups", where the owner can Stop one), the enrolments with his words, and this session in Activity. Never send him to the admin to enrol or prepare.

1. **Bind** as `skills/triage-mail/SKILL.md` section 0 (one project per session).
2. **Enrol, only when his words ask for it.** A phrase that names clients or a segment to put in a follow-up (`подготовь follow-up молчащим клиентам проекта <slug>`, `поставь в follow-up <client>`) is an enrolment order; a phrase that only asks for the due steps (`подготовь follow-up проекта <slug>`) is not: skip to step 3.
   - Who: the clients he named, or for a segment `hub sequences candidates <slug> --json` (read only: we wrote last, long enough ago, nothing open, no do-not-contact) narrowed to what he said. Cap 30; say what was left. Nobody fits: say so and enrol nobody.
   - Each one: `hub sequences enrol <slug> --client <email> --phrase "<his words, verbatim>"` (add `--sequence <id>` when he named one; else the hub picks the one that fits). The hub refuses an `enrol` without `--phrase` in a session, and the guard flags it. A refusal (an open enrollment, do not contact, no sequence fits) is that client's row `skipped:<reason>`; go on.
   - Then go on to step 3: a client whose first wait has already passed is due at once.
3. **Read what is due:** `hub sequences due <slug> --json`. Each row gives `enrollmentId`, the client, `step` of `steps`, `lastStep`, `replyTo` (our newest message: subject, `mailRef`), `stepRow` (the step's purpose, angle and length from the file), `optOut` (step 2 and later), `languages`, and `changedSinceEnrolment`. Read the sequence file `projects/<slug>/sequences/<id>.md` once for its Goal, its "Do not" lines and its `max_words` (front matter; 90 when absent). Nothing due: say "по проекту <slug> follow-up не нужен" and stop. Cap 30 rows; say what was left.
4. **Per client, before any text** (`skills/triage-mail/references/per-client.md` P1 to P3):
   - P2 history: the client's inbound since `replyTo.sentAt`, and a bounce (a delivery failure to this address) since then.
   - P1 profile (read only): Notion's do-not-contact or do-not-follow-up, Relationship, Lead Status.
   - Then put what you read through the stop rule: `echo '<facts>' | hub sequences judge <slug> --id <enrollmentId>` with `{"lastInboundAt":...,"bounced":...,"optedOut":...,"outOfOfficeUntil":...,"doNotContact":...,"doNotFollowUp":...,"relationship":...,"leadStatus":...}` (only what you read; leave the rest out). `"finish"` or `"delay"` in its answer: the hub has already stopped or delayed it, report the row as `skipped:<reason>` and draft nothing. `"continue"`: go on.
   - A message that only says "remove me" or "stop" is an opt-out (`"optedOut":true`). An automatic out-of-office reply gives `outOfOfficeUntil` (its return date).
5. **Write** (P3 to P4): the step's purpose and angle are the brief, never a body. New value from that client's thread and the project's knowledge (facts only from `knowledge_search`, else `[Owner: ...]`). No "just checking in", no "following up on my last email", no guilt, no invented deadline. At most the file's `max_words` words, the opt-out line included. Step 2 and later end with the file's `optOut` sentence, word for word in the client's language. `lastStep`: the closing line the file gives, then nothing more.
6. **Draft** (P5): one unsent `draft_write` (hub) on our message `replyTo.mailRef`; it adds nothing to your text but the signature (never type a sign-off: `own_signoff`). A rerun is idempotent through the hub's record: `already_drafted` means this step's draft exists, so skip the client (not an error). If no reply can be made, report `skipped: no-message-to-reply` and give the text in chat.
7. **Record** (P6): `hub record-draft` as `skills/triage-mail/SKILL.md` section 2 step 5, with `"skill":"sequence-drafts"`, `mailClass` of our message, `"sequence":{"enrollmentId":<id>,"step":<step>}` and the run's one `batchKey` in `context` (`seq-<date>-<time>-<3 random chars>`, the same on every record of this session, so Activity shows the session once). The hub refuses a step over another open draft of the client: report that row `skipped: open-draft`. A step is **never sent by the hub** (`hub send` refuses it in every mode); the owner sends it from Mail.
8. **No Notion write** in this skill: the profile and the log stay as they are.
9. **Report** in the format of `skills/README.md` ("Report"); `действие` per row is `drafted`, `skipped:replied`, `skipped:bounced`, `skipped:opted_out`, `skipped:closed`, `skipped:excluded`, `skipped:out_of_office` or `skipped:<code>`. `changedSinceEnrolment` true: one P2 line ("файл follow-up <id> изменился после записи клиента"). End with "Черновики лежат в Mail, ничего не отправлено."

Never: send, enrol without his words in this session, pause, resume, write Notion, read another project, or draft a client the judge stopped.
