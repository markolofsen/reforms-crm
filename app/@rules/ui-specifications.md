# UI specifications

Scope: how UI behaviour is written down so a test can hold it. Adapted from the shared "executable UI specifications" page; there is no scenario workbench here, so the contract is carried by Playwright flows (against a production build) and Vitest over pure helpers.

## Rules

1. MUST: every screen has named scenarios for its states, and each runs in a real browser flow or a unit test: `drafts: empty`, `drafts: error`, `drafts: mutation rejected`. A state with no scenario is not done.
1a. MUST: the shell and the home have these named scenarios: `status line: clear`, `status line: attention`, `status line: blocked` (the worst fact said once, at most one link, and the to-review count not repeated); `shell: one project` (no switcher, `/` lands on Mail, Overview in the nav and a page of "Needs you" or "Everything works" with the parts folded under "All parts", no project table: P63, P70); `shell: two projects` (the switcher under the brand header, Overview first and the entry, its project table above "Needs you"); `nav: nested` (P70, P75: Sending, Checks, Mail, Agent, Connections and This Mac in the sub-nav of every Settings pane, a menu below 640px, a pane's dot on its tab; never in the sidebar, open or rail, nor on the phone bar; the phone bar is Overview, Mail, Activity, Settings and More, with Messengers (P71 M3), Knowledge and Help in More; Messengers' Chats and Settings in the sub-nav of both its pages); `theme: no flash` (a cold load with Light, Dark and System saved shows the right theme on first paint and no hydration message; the choice survives a reload; System follows `prefers-color-scheme`); `activity: one timeline` (P73: the decisions newest first under a heading per local day, one line per fold of one client and outcome on one day, no session heading, no `?run=` and no detail pane; at 360 px a row is two lines of the same table; the View counts are the server's; a draft row opens its card; `?client=` says whose timeline it is, with Show all).
2. MUST: components and hooks are pure with respect to their inputs. No `Date.now()`, `new Date()` or `Math.random()` in render; take time from one injectable clock (`lib/clock.ts`).
3. MUST: logic that can be pure (route parsing, label maps, filters, guards) is a pure function with a Vitest test beside it (`*.test.ts` in `src`).
4. MUST: the e2e suite runs at 360, 768 and 1280 px; a screen has no sideways page scroll and 44px touch targets below 1024px.
4a. MUST: a screen shows only what the next decision needs (P44 rubric 2). Gated mechanically: no screen has more than 36 tab stops in its first viewport at any width (44 while drafts are being selected, when every row's checkbox is a stop on purpose), on a phone the first draft of Mail sits at most 200 px down, the status line included (`CONTROL_CAP` and `FIRST_ROW_MAX_PHONE` in `e2e/a-layout.spec.ts`); selecting a row does not move the first row; at 1280 a row's subject is not cut below 40 characters. Past the cap, show less (move something to the pane or view it belongs to); do not raise the number. A Settings pane is never folded to pass it (P75): Checks, all its rules in view, measured under the cap before it was built. Not gated, because no stable count exists: colours and type sizes per screen, copy, icons and badges without a job. Those are scored by hand against the nine-point rubric (one primary action, show less, hierarchy by space, plain words, designed states, quiet, reachable, honest, task cost), kept in the owner's plan records.
5. MUST: keyboard paths are part of the contract: the conversation keys are two maps (P127 C2): the list keys `LIST_KEYS` (and `SELECTION_KEYS` where the channel selects) in `components/conversation/list/keys.ts`, shared by Mail and Messengers, and each channel's action keys (Mail's `MAIL_ACTION_KEYS` in `features/mail/list/keys.ts`); the `?` popover lists the rows that register on the screen: `j`/`k` move, `Enter` goes into the card and `Esc` back, `c` Checked for Send all Checked (on opens the review dialog, off at once), `x` and `Shift A` select, `r` reply in Mail (batch drafts), `d`/`s`/`h` done, skip, hold, `u` undo, `1 2 3` the version, `/` search, `Shift Enter` Send: it opens the review dialog), `g o` / `g d` / `g m` / `g a` / `g k` / `g s` / `g h` go between screens (parents only; a child page is one Tab away in the sub-nav); a typing field, a menu or a dialog switches them off. One shared hook (`useHotkeys` of `@hub/ui/use-hotkey`) owns the typing guard, the modifier guard and the chord window, and `hooks/use-hotkeys` the remembered off switch; the chords are derived from `SECTIONS`.
5b. MUST: the review card is a pure function of one of 14 states, one per row of the table in `features/mail/draft/card-state.ts` (`CARD_STATES`; the states of a draft from research 16 section 7, and `follow_up`, a step of a follow-up sequence; P105 removed `confirming`, since Send is confirmed in the review dialog): each state names its one primary button, at most two secondaries and whether Checked can be set (`canCheck`). Send is never disabled: it opens the dialog, which names what stands in the way. A new state is a row in that table and in `test/card-state.test.ts`.
5d. MUST: a reversible move says so and offers the way back: Done, Skip and Hold raise a toast with Undo (one draft, or one toast for a whole selection, whose Undo puts every draft back), and the Client menu does the same for a flag; only an irreversible one asks first: Send (and Send all Checked, Unlink, Hide) in the review dialog of rule 5f, Do not contact in place. Every one of the 14 card states is reachable and has a scenario: `unreadable` (Mail cannot be read, with Read again) is not a state the list can fake, so it is driven by a patched Mail read.
5a. MUST: every screen has a deep-link scenario: a cold load of its real path (`/mail/c/42?project=lisbon&filter=all`) and a reload keep project, section, filter and the selected subject (`e2e/b-routing.spec.ts`).
5c. MUST: every route has a hydration scenario: a cold load at each width shows no console error, warning or hydration message, and the server HTML is the loading state, never data, a viewport branch or a stored value (`e2e/c-console.spec.ts`). A component that reads the viewport, storage, the cookie or the clock follows `state-and-data.md` rule 11.
5e. MUST: Mail and Messengers use the shared conversation frame for the same interaction contract: the header stays fixed, history owns the main scroll, and the composer is docked below it. A long composer body may scroll inside its own body, but its action row, errors and recovery controls stay visible at the bottom. Do not add a second outer padding or a second history scroll container. Incoming and outgoing messages use the shared message component, with direction expressed by alignment and semantic tokens rather than feature-local bubble rules. Manual replies use the shared plain-text editor with no formatting buttons until P93's RTF path lands (P123 U0, `ui-composition.md` rule 8); the editor owns its modifier shortcuts and prevents a shell shortcut before it runs. The reply composer rests closed (P128 X1): a person with no draft shows one action row (`DraftActionRow`): Wrote you is Prepare reply (primary) · No reply needed · Write it myself, and when the agent judged them (`row.agent`, P128 X2) its line is said and No reply needed is the primary; Waiting and Done show Write again. Writing opens the composer in place with focus in the body, also when there is no incoming message (with its explanation); its body never holds a placeholder signature, and a missing brand is asked at Save, not before typing. When Mail resolves a brand from incoming headers, the composer shows that brand as a locked context with a tooltip; the selector is interactive only when the brand still needs an owner choice.
5f. MUST: a decision that cannot be taken back is reviewed in one dialog, once (P105): `ReviewDialog` of `@hub/ui/product/review-dialog`. It lists every problem in one scroll, blockers first (`orderItems`), each with its rule in words and, for a blocker, its fix as a button; then one Cancel and one confirm. A warning is read and accepted by the confirm (no per-item Understood, no switch); a blocker keeps the confirm off and the dialog says why (`reviewGate`); a fact of the setting (test mode) is the calm first line, never a warning. Esc and Cancel do nothing; focus starts on Cancel while a blocker remains, on the confirm otherwise; a confirm within `REVIEW_DWELL_MS` of opening is ignored, so a held key never presses it. The dialog is transient state, never in the address. The unsent message's buttons are one row, `DraftActionRow` of `@hub/ui/product/draft-action-row`, in every composer: reversible actions at the start, the one primary at the end; its Send opens the dialog and never sends by itself.
6. SHOULD: an e2e flow fails on any console error.
7. SHOULD: a handoff attaches evidence: the commands run, the viewports opened, one screenshot per state changed.
8. Utility CSS must be static strings (see `stylesheets-and-class-lists.md`); a test may read class names, so they are never built at runtime.
