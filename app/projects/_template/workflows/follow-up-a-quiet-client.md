# Follow up a quiet client

### Goal

A brief follow-up that makes it easy to answer, drafted once per quiet period.

### Input

A person whose last message in the Conversation log is ours and is older than 5 working days.

### Steps

1. Read the Conversation summary and the last two log lines to see what we asked.
2. Draft two or three sentences: remind them of the open question, offer one easy way to answer, no pressure.
3. If the last follow-up in the log is less than 10 working days old: do nothing.

### Do not

- Follow up a person with Do not contact ticked.
- Send. Draft only.
- Follow up a Priority grade person without telling the owner. An empty Grade is Standard. Leads have no Grade or Do not contact: read them from the linked person if Client is set.
- Write the draft anywhere but the mail client's drafts or the report without a person's OK.

### Done when

A draft exists or the report says why not.

### Report back

The draft and the date of the last contact.
