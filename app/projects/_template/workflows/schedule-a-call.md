# Schedule a call

### Goal

A scheduling reply that settles the time in one more message.

### Input

The email, and the team calendar if it is available to you.

### Steps

1. Read the proposed times and the person's time zone if known (Conversation summary).
2. If calendar access is available: check the owner's free slots. If not: say a person will confirm the time and offer to take their preferred slots.
3. Draft: confirm one option or offer two to three slots with the time zone written out.
4. Do not create a calendar event. A human does that after confirming.

### Do not

- Create or change calendar events.
- Send. Draft only.
- Invent availability.

### Done when

A draft exists that either confirms or offers specific times with a time zone.

### Report back

The draft and the slots offered.
