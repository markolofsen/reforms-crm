# Answered enquiry, no reply

## Goal

Two or three short follow-ups after our first answer, each adding one thing the person can use, then a polite close.

## Steps

| # | after (days) | purpose | adds | knowledge topics |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2 | Make sure our answer reached them and invite the next question | one concrete detail about what they asked, not repeated from our answer | the subject of their enquiry |
| 2 | 3 | Offer an easy next step | one option: a short call, a document, or an example | next steps, contact |
| 3 | 5 | Close the file politely | how to come back later, in one line | contact |

## Last step

Say plainly that we will not write again about this enquiry and how to reach us when the time is right. No guilt, no pressure.

## Do not

- Write "just checking in", "bumping this up" or "following up on my last email" without anything new.
- Repeat our first answer.
- State a price, date or term that is not in the knowledge base: use an `[Owner: ...]` placeholder.
- Go past about 90 words.

## Opt-out line

If you would rather I stop writing, just say so.
