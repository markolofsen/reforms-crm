# Proposal sent, no reply

## Goal

Help the client decide on the proposal we sent: answer the likely question, offer one alternative, then close the file.

## Steps

| # | after (days) | purpose | adds | knowledge topics |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 3 | Answer the question clients most often have about this kind of proposal | one fact that removes a common doubt | terms, process |
| 2 | 4 | Offer one alternative or a smaller first step | a second option they can say yes to | options, pricing |
| 3 | 7 | Close the file politely | how to pick it up again later | contact |

## Last step

Say we will close the proposal for now, that it can be reopened, and how. One line of value, no pressure.

## Do not

- Write "just checking in" or ask "did you get my email?".
- Change a price or a term in the proposal: the owner decides that.
- Attach anything; name the document instead.

If you would rather I stop writing, just say so.
